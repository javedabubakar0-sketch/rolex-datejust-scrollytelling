INTERNAL MECHANISM

CALIBRE 3235

Fourteen patents. Superior energy efficiency. Self-winding mechanical movement offering unprecedented reliability.

Chronergy Escapement

Rolex's patented geometry optimizes the Swiss lever escapement. The thickness of the pallet stones has been reduced by half, while the gear teeth of the escape wheel are skeletonized to reduce inertia. Made of nickel-phosphorus, it is entirely insensitive to magnetic interference.

+15% Overall Escapement Energy Efficiency

Blue Parachrom Hairspring

Manufactured by Rolex from an exclusive paramagnetic alloy of niobium and zirconium. Unaffected by magnetic fields, it remains up to 10 times more precise than a traditional hairspring in the event of shocks. Its Rolex overcoil ensures regularity in every position.

10X Greater Shock Resistance & Stability

High-Capacity Barrel Architecture

Thanks to the improved efficiency of the Chronergy escapement and reduced barrel wall thickness, Rolex engineers accommodated a mainspring with significantly greater capacity, extending the power reserve from 48 to approximately 70 hours.

70 HRS Autonomous Weekend-Proof Power Reserve

Paraflex Shock Absorbers

Designed and patented by Rolex, the Paraflex shock absorber system increases the shock resistance of the balance wheel and vital escapement components by up to 50%, guaranteeing peace of mind through athletic and daily wear.

50% Increased Balance Staff Impact Protection

EIGHT DECADES OF PRESTIGE

THE DATEJUST CHRONOLOGY

A journey through milestones that transformed modern horology forever.

1945

The Birth of an Icon

Rolex celebrates its 40th anniversary with the launch of the Datejust: the first self-winding waterproof chronometer wristwatch with an automatic date aperture.

1953

The Cyclops Magnifier

Rolex patents the Cyclops lens on the sapphire crystal, magnifying the date by 2.5 times for instant clarity. It remains a global hallmark of Rolex design.

1977

Quickset Date Mechanism

Calibre 3035 is introduced, incorporating the quickset date mechanism allowing the date to be advanced independently without rotating the hour hand 24 hours.

2016

Calibre 3235 Generation

A triumphant mechanical evolution. Calibre 3235 debuts with the Chronergy escapement, 70-hour autonomy, and the strict Superlative Chronometer standard.
